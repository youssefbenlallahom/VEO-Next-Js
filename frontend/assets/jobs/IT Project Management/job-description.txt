Internal Job Title: IT Project Manager
Position Type: Execution
Structure/Committee: IT / Board
Reports to: IT PMO Team Leader
Location: Tunis / Sousse
POSITION INTERFACE
Cross-functional collaboration within Veoworldwide: collaborates with the IT Director, subordinate team members, IT, COMIT, Payroll and Personnel Administration, HR, Site Manager, all Veoworldwide users.
Cross-functional collaboration within NOZ: collaborates with IT France, collaborates with IT Veoworldwide.
PURPOSE OF THE POSITION
Experienced IT project manager to manage multiple projects and ensure oversight of all workflows. In this role, you will use Waterfall (V) or Agile IT project management methodologies and provide leadership, direction, and integrate activities with other IT departments to ensure successful implementation and support.
KEY POSITION RESPONSIBILITIES
Operational Responsibilities:

Prioritize tasks, set deadlines, and assign staff to various deliverables so that each project can start with minimal loss of time and resources
Lead and motivate a team composed of various stakeholders from all NOZ departments (sales, marketing, logistics, IT services, HR, Finance, etc.)
Collaborate with other business or IT team leaders engaged in problem-solving
Ensure that team members follow the work schedule and strategy
Foster cooperation within the team, help PMO Team Leader anticipate challenges and intervene in alert and crisis situations by implementing solutions

Reporting Responsibilities:

Use effective PM methodology tools such as Jira or Tempo to capture all data concerning project performance
Provide regular reports on project progress, milestones, key performance indicators, risks, and budget
Support IT PMO KPIs and other efficiency reports
Communicate effectively with all parties involved in the project using standardized methods and approaches such as RACI

Internal Client Responsibilities:

Ensure that standards are met and clearly defined throughout the quality execution process of each project
Improve new and pre-existing processes
Responsible for successful achievement of SLA and set objectives

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
The direct manager communicates monthly, quarterly, and annual key performance indicators to the employee.
Operational KPIs:

Support achievement of PMO team operational objectives
Meet requested deadlines for LDs where responsible as Project Manager
Study Monitoring - compliance with delivery deadlines (number of late projects <5%); completed project compared to estimate (maximum deviation of 10%)

POSITION REQUIREMENTS
Respect internal policies, regulations, and company work procedures:
The employee will rigorously follow the company's internal policies and work procedures, as regularly communicated and/or available on the company intranet/website and OBSYS application.
The employee will comply with the company's internal regulations and individual employment contract.
University Degree/Foreign Languages/Technical Expertise:

Education level: higher education / engineering cycle
Foreign languages: French, English - minimum B2
University degree in computer science, information technology, business administration, or related field. A master's degree is preferable
Communication and interpersonal skills
Project management certifications such as PMP, PRINCE2, or others are a plus

Experience:
Minimum of 5 years of experience in project management, as Project Manager, with a focus on IT projects.
Key Skills:

Strong understanding of project management methodologies such as Agile or Waterfall
Clear use of tools and experience managing a project > 3 years: Jira Atlassian / MS Azure DevOps / Microsoft Project or similar
Experience working with cross-functional teams and stakeholders at all levels of the organization
