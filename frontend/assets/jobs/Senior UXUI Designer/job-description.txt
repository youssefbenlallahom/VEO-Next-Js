Job Title: Senior UX/UI Designer
Position Type: Full-Time
Reports to: Head of Design
Location: Tunis (Hybrid)
POSITION OVERVIEW
We are seeking a talented Senior UX/UI Designer to join our dynamic product team. In this role, you will be responsible for creating exceptional user experiences across our digital platforms, from initial concept through final implementation. You'll work closely with product managers, developers, and stakeholders to design intuitive, accessible, and visually compelling interfaces that solve real user problems.
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
Design & User Experience:

Lead end-to-end design processes for web and mobile applications, from user research and wireframing to high-fidelity prototypes and final UI design
Conduct user research, usability testing, and gather insights to inform design decisions and validate solutions
Create user flows, journey maps, personas, and information architecture to ensure optimal user experiences
Design responsive interfaces that work seamlessly across different devices and screen sizes
Develop and maintain comprehensive design systems, component libraries, and style guides to ensure consistency across all products
Collaborate with developers to ensure accurate implementation of designs and provide design specifications and assets

Collaboration & Leadership:

Work closely with product managers to understand business requirements and translate them into effective design solutions
Partner with engineering teams to ensure technical feasibility and smooth handoff of design deliverables
Present and articulate design concepts and rationale to stakeholders at all levels
Mentor junior designers and contribute to building a strong design culture within the organization
Participate in design critiques and provide constructive feedback to team members

Strategy & Innovation:

Stay current with design trends, tools, and best practices in UX/UI design
Advocate for user-centered design principles throughout the organization
Identify opportunities for design improvements and propose innovative solutions
Contribute to the evolution of design processes and methodologies within the team

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
Design Quality:

User satisfaction scores and usability testing results
Design system adoption rate across products
Time from concept to implementation
Number of design iterations required before approval

Collaboration & Delivery:

On-time delivery of design deliverables (>95% of projects)
Stakeholder satisfaction ratings
Cross-functional collaboration effectiveness
Design handoff quality score from development team

Impact & Innovation:

Measurable improvements in key product metrics (conversion rates, task completion rates, etc.)
Implementation of design system components
Contribution to design process improvements

POSITION REQUIREMENTS
Education & Languages:

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Design, HCI, or related field (or equivalent practical experience)
Languages: French and English - minimum B2 level
Strong written and verbal communication skills

Experience:

Minimum 5 years of experience in UX/UI design for digital products (web and mobile)
Proven track record of shipping successful products from concept to launch
Experience working in agile/scrum environments
Portfolio demonstrating strong design thinking, problem-solving abilities, and visual design skills

Technical Skills:

Expert proficiency in design tools: Figma, Adobe XD, or Sketch
Strong understanding of design systems and component-based design
Proficiency in prototyping tools (Figma, ProtoPie, Principle, or similar)
Knowledge of HTML/CSS fundamentals (ability to collaborate effectively with developers)
Experience with user research and testing tools (UserTesting, Hotjar, Maze, or similar)
Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG) and inclusive design principles

Key Competencies:

Strong visual design skills with attention to typography, color theory, and layout
Excellent problem-solving abilities and analytical thinking
User-centered design mindset with empathy for end users
Ability to balance user needs with business goals and technical constraints
Strong presentation and storytelling skills
Collaborative team player with the ability to work across different departments
Self-motivated with ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously
Open to feedback and continuous learning

Nice to Have:

Experience with motion design and micro-interactions
Knowledge of front-end development (React, Vue.js, etc.)
Experience with design ops and workflow optimization
Background in branding or visual identity design
Certification in UX design or related field
